# Original art

Built-in ImageGen, 2026-09-13 KST. Original PNG preserved at `art-source/key-art.png`; format-only WebP encoding at `assets/key-art.webp`, 1536×1024. This project uses original adult characters Lea and the Lenskeeper, not characters from an existing franchise. Model geometry and museum materials are authored in code. No source artwork is referenced from outside the repository at runtime.

Six original museum paintings were generated as a 1536×1024 atlas, preserved at `art-source/paintings.png` and encoded without cropping or retouching as `assets/paintings.webp`. The six atlas cells are sampled by real wall-mounted geometry; their data URL is bundled for offline WebGL loading. They are decorative paintings, separate from the six collectible 3D relics.
